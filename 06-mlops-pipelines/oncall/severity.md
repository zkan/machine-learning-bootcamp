# Severity

Use this table for the Fresh and Good classroom incident. The shop is fictional. The point is to pick a level and say what a customer would notice.

| Level | What a customer would notice | A matching situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV-1 | Every score the shop might act on is wrong | The form is unusable for the whole customer list |
| SEV-2 | One district or one plan is wrong, and the rest still score | Scores for a single district dropped together |
| SEV-3 | The shop can still score customers | The monitor page is blank, or a chart failed to draw |
| SEV-4 | Nothing a customer would notice today | A typo in a label, or a report that will be late |

Write the level in the incident channel in the first message, then update it if the facts change.
