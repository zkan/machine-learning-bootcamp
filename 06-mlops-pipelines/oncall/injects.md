# Inject cards

Read one card out loud. The pair answers in one minute. The answers are not on this page.

1. The on-call lead's laptop battery is dead. Who tells the shop what is happening?
2. A stakeholder asks to turn the model off in the next five minutes. What do you say, and what do you do first?
3. After a restart, scores are no longer all zero, and nobody can say why. Do you close the incident?
4. Someone asks which person wrote the bad task. What do you say in a blameless review?
