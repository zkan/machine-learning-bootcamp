# Presenter guide

You have about two of the eight demo minutes for the business story. Use these four headings. One or two sentences each.

## Problem

Who at Fresh and Good uses the score, and what do they do differently when the score is high?

## Cost of errors

What does a wasted voucher cost, and what does a missed churner cost? Use the Day 1 cost card if your team still has those two numbers. If you do not, say that the numbers are a classroom assumption.

## ROI

What would have to be true for the voucher program to pay for itself? Name the cost and the benefit in baht, even if the benefit is a guess you label as a guess.

## Risks

Name one way the score can be wrong on demo day. The skew hunt and the drift page are both fair answers. Say what you will do when that happens. Pause the form, roll the alias back, or retrain. Pick one.
