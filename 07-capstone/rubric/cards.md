# Rubric cards

Score each card 0, 1, or 2. 0 means missing. 1 means present with a gap a teammate can name. 2 means a classmate can rerun it from the README.

## Problem

The team can say who is scored and what a wrong score would make the shop do.

## Reproducibility

A second `dvc repro` does no work, and the team can point at code, data, params, and the environment.

## Registry

The form loads `fresh-and-good-churn` at alias `champion`. The team can say which version that alias points at.

## Pipeline

The plain runner, or the Airflow dag, has registered a model that passed the gate.

## Monitor

The monitor page opens. The team can say what they would do if a column crossed the PSI line.

## Story

The demo fits in 8 minutes and names one risk.
